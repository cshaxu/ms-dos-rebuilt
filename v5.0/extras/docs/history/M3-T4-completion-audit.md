## Investigation and review

The sandbox denied CreateDesktop. The same isolated helper ran under approved
execution permissions. Initial product probes waited without starting a guest.
Stack candidate offsets mapped to base_sync_platform_event_wait_many and
console_broker_reader; candidates alone were not treated as an unwound trace.
The product source defines initial STOPPED state and monitor `start`. Submitting
that command through the owned isolated console produced Running and guest
artifacts. No change to DOS source was needed.

Reviewed preparer, FAT reader, helper, verification logic and retained results.
The helper's exit zero reports cleanup/isolation, not successful guest behavior;
the separate guest-file checks establish the smoke result. The test forces
process termination and does not prove graceful shutdown. Fixture padding,
original IO.SYS/MSDOS.SYS and narrow smoke scope remain explicit limitations.
